Two sourced essays on physical limits

One works out how far a wave can fall in frequency before it stops being sound. The other works out how dense light can get before the vacuum breaks down. Each states what is measured, what is inferred, and what is disputed.

Sound How sound turns into something else Below a certain frequency pressure stops carrying the wave and buoyancy takes over. Between them is a band nothing can travel through — and one process that skips it. Light How light turns into something else Focused light stays lighter than water until the vacuum breaks down. Eight thresholds later, one neutron decides how an atom behaves near absolute zero.
